About us · since 1962

One caring relationship can change a life

For more than 60 years, Community Club has paired DC public school students with volunteer tutors, one-on-one and free of charge.

Our story

Over 60 years in DC

Community Club was founded in 1962 on a simple belief: every DC student deserves a caring adult in their corner. Since the 1970s we've met at New York Avenue Presbyterian Church.

In 1983 we began honoring a Tutor of the Year. In the 1990s we added a college scholarship. Today the New York Avenue Education Foundation helps us receive Combined Federal Campaign gifts.
