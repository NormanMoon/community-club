Free tutoring in Washington, DC · since 1962

One student. One tutor. One whole school year.

Free one-on-one tutoring for DC public school students in grades 6–11, every Thursday at New York Avenue Presbyterian Church.

Become a tutor Enroll a student
How it works

Three simple steps

  1. Apply

    Tutors fill out a short online form. Families register their student.

  2. Get matched

    Each student is paired with one volunteer tutor for the whole school year.

  3. Meet every Thursday

    Thursdays, 6:30–8:00 pm, at New York Avenue Presbyterian Church.

For tutors

No teaching experience needed. We provide orientation and support all year.
