What Community Club gives you

Your own tutor

The same adult volunteer every week, all year.

Any subject

Homework, test prep, writing, math, science: whatever you need most.

Events

Bowling night, a Wizards game and the year-end celebration.

What we ask of you

  1. Come every Thursday, and tell your tutor or the directors in advance if you can't.
  2. Bring your homework and know what you want to work on.
  3. Respect your tutor and the space.
  4. Tell your tutor what's working and what isn't.

College timetable

Plan for college, grade by grade

9th grade

Meet your counselor, build study habits, try activities. Grades start counting now.

10th grade

Take challenging classes, take the PSAT 10, start a list of colleges you like.

11th grade

PSAT in October, SAT in spring (free at school), ask teachers for recommendations, start your essay.

12th grade

Apply by November–January, file the FAFSA from October 1, apply for DC TAG, decide by May 1.

Already in college

Community Club college scholarship

Current Community Club college students may qualify for a scholarship each semester by keeping a 2.5 GPA or higher and attending the yearly CC college meeting.
